About EPA

Supporting our students, teachers, and school

The EPA Mission

Envision Parents in Action (EPA) is a non-profit organization comprised of eager, supportive, and active parents, guardians, and grandparents who want to help structure a meaningful learning environment for our students and provide support for our teachers.

Teachers and administrators are welcome to join the organization. EPA members organize events, conduct fundraisers, recruit volunteers, and promote the culture of ESA.

What We Do

  • Events — Family fun nights, cultural heritage celebrations, spirit days, and more
  • Fundraising — Book fairs, restaurant nights, Shop & Earn programs, and spirit gear sales
  • Volunteer coordination — Stock the Shelves, teacher appreciation, classroom helpers, and event support
  • Community building — Connecting families with teachers, staff, and each other

Who Can Join

EPA membership is open to all parents, guardians, grandparents, teachers, and administrators at Envision Science Academy. Membership is free — your time and enthusiasm are what matter most.
