About EPA

Supporting our students, teachers, and school

Our Mission

Supporting every Eagle's journey

Envision Parents in Action (EPA) is a non-profit organization comprised of eager, supportive, and active parents, guardians, and grandparents who want to help structure a meaningful learning environment for our students and provide support for our teachers.

Teachers and administrators are welcome to join the organization. EPA members organize events, conduct fundraisers, recruit volunteers, and promote the culture of ESA.

What We Do

EPA brings families together to support students, teachers, and school culture throughout the year.

🎉

Events

Family fun nights, cultural heritage celebrations, spirit days, and more that bring our community together.

💰

Fundraising

Book fairs, restaurant nights, Shop & Earn programs, and spirit gear sales that benefit our school.

🤝

Volunteer Coordination

Stock the Shelves, teacher appreciation, classroom helpers, and event support when families are needed most.

🏫

Community Building

Connecting families with teachers, staff, and each other to strengthen the ESA experience.
