Get Involved

Membership, spirit gear, fundraising, volunteering, and more

Membership

EPA membership is free and open to all parents, guardians, grandparents, teachers, and administrators at Envision Science Academy. Join us to stay informed about events, volunteer opportunities, and ways to support our school.

Contact our Membership Chair, Amy Toomey, at membership@weareepa.org.

ESA Spirit Shop

We have two ways for you to shop for ESA spirit gear:

  1. EPA Spirit Shop — Spirit T-shirts, blankets, towels, water bottles, pens, decals & more. Check the events calendar for upcoming spirit shop dates.
  2. Capital Promotions — Sweatshirts, outerwear, and other items through our local vendor.

Questions? Email spiritgear@weareepa.org.

Volunteer Opportunities

There are many ways to lend a hand throughout the school year:

  • Stock the Shelves — Donate supplies to restock teacher closets. See the calendar for drop-off dates.
  • Teacher Appreciation Week — Sign up to bring treats, help with lunches, or show our teachers some love.
  • Event helpers — Family fun nights, cultural events, book fairs, and spirit days always need volunteers.
  • Classroom & recess support — Help in classrooms or during recess throughout the year.

Interested in volunteering? Check the events calendar or contact us to find out where help is needed.
